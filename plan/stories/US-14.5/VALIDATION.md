### Verdict: PASS WITH NOTES

Session-backed `getCurrentUser()`, deny-by-default allowlist middleware, every-request `active` (before `role`), `requireActive()` / `requireOperator()`, cookie idle ≤ 7 days, production fallback throw, pending identity from the server seam, and the skip-if-missing operator seed match the frozen contract. Notes are unproven live SQL flips, no spend/operator product endpoints yet (helpers are the floor), and `next build` throwing when `AUTH_DEV_FALLBACK` is set locally — none of which this story is required to close.

**Blockers:** none.
