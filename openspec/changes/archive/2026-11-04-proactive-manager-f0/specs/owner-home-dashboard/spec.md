## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Automation notifications on Hoy
Hoy SHALL surface unread automation notification items (pending-approval batches / reminders) in the feed when present, using plain-language EN/ES copy, without spending or publishing.

#### Scenario: Pending approval notification visible
- **WHEN** an automation Hoy notification exists for the owner
- **THEN** Hoy shows it in the feed (Needs OK or a notifications strip) with a path to review
